AVRA LOGO KIT
Growth that flows.

FOLDERS
01-Primary-Wordmark      The main logo. Use this whenever space allows.
02-Secondary-Lockups     Wordmark with tagline (stacked or horizontal) or with the descriptor line.
03-Submark               The A from the wordmark, for small spaces, avatars and stamps.
04-Favicon-App-Icons     favicon.svg, favicon.ico, 16/32/48 px, Apple touch icon, Android icons.
05-Social                Profile pictures, Open Graph share image, LinkedIn banner, Story background.

FORMATS
SVG  Vector master files. Use for web, print and any resizing. Text is converted to outlines.
PNG  Transparent background, in several widths. Use in documents, slides and social tools.
JPG  On a solid brand background with clear space. Use where transparency is not supported.

COLOURWAYS
graphite  #111111  default logo colour on light backgrounds
white     #FFFFFF  on dark backgrounds and photos
black     #000000  single-colour print
paper     #F2F1EE  on Graphite or Signal backgrounds
Signal    #D9372B  accent only; the logo appears in Paper on Signal, never Signal on light backgrounds.

TYPE
Inter Tight (tagline and headlines), Geist Mono (labels and descriptor).

CLEAR SPACE AND SIZE
Keep empty space around the logo of at least half the wordmark's height.
Minimum width: 90 px on screen, 25 mm in print. Below that, use the submark.

DON'T
Stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or gradients, recolour outside the palette, or rebuild the letters.

NOTE
The vector files were traced from the supplied logo image. If the original designer's master file exists,
compare against it before large-format print.
